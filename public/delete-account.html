Delete your Mill Mate account

Mill Mate, the purchase and order-tracking app by Apronics

Mill Mate accounts are created by each company's administrator for its staff. You can delete your account and personal data at any time, whether or not you still work with that company.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Mill Mate and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings › Delete My Account.
  3. Enter your password, type DELETE, and tap Delete account. It is deleted at once and you are signed out.

If you are your company's only Main Admin, make another user Main Admin first, or contact us to close the company's account.

Can't sign in any more?

Ask your company's Mill Mate administrator, who can delete the account directly, or email hello@apranex.tech with the subject "Mill Mate account deletion", your company code (if you have one), your username and your name. We confirm it is you, delete the account within 30 days, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your login (username and password).
  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number, department and profile picture.
  • Your notification and WhatsApp alert settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Orders, approvals, delivery records and attached documents (for example LR copies and proof of receipt) are your company's business records. They are kept as long as the company needs them for its accounts and legal records, and may still show the name of the person who created or approved them.
  • Activity log entries (who changed what, and when) are kept for the same reason.
  • Database backups are overwritten on a rolling basis; deleted account data disappears from backups within 90 days.
