Mill Mate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Mill Mate is a purchase and order-tracking app for mills and factories, provided by Apronics ("we", "us"). Companies subscribe to Mill Mate for their staff. Each company's administrator creates the accounts for that company's employees; the public cannot sign up.

Each company's data is kept in its own separate database. The company decides who in it can use Mill Mate and what they can see; Apronics runs the service on the company's behalf.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, username, work email, mobile number, department, role, and an optional profile picture.
  • Order information: material requests, quantities, purchase orders, delivery dates, status changes and remarks.
  • Photos and documents you attach: for example purchase documents, lorry receipts (LR copies) and proof-of-receipt photos. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to attach a file.
  • Supplier contact details (company name, contact person, phone, WhatsApp number, email, address) and the WhatsApp messages exchanged with suppliers about availability and delivery dates.
  • Activity records: an audit log of actions in the app (who changed what and when), used for accountability within the company.

We do not collect location, contacts from your phone, advertising identifiers, or browsing activity outside the app.

How we use it

  • To run each company's purchasing process: approvals, purchase orders, supplier communication, deliveries and receipts.
  • To show notifications about orders inside the app.
  • To keep the app secure, for example signing you in and limiting repeated wrong passwords.
  • To give support when a company's administrator asks for it. Support staff can open a company's data only after that company's administrator allows it, and every visit is recorded.

We do not sell your information, show advertising, or use it for any purpose other than providing Mill Mate to your company.

Who can see it

  • Authorised Mill Mate users within your own company, according to their role. Other companies using Mill Mate cannot see your company's data.
  • Suppliers receive only the order details your company sends them (items, quantities, purchase order number) through WhatsApp.
  • Our service providers, who process data only on our behalf: Amazon Web Services (servers and file storage) and Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business messaging with suppliers).

Storage and security

Data is stored on Amazon Web Services servers and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Files are kept in private encrypted storage and are opened only through short-lived links. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.

How long we keep it

Order and audit records are kept for as long as your company needs them for its business and legal records. When a user leaves, the company's administrator disables or deletes the account, or the user deletes it themselves.

Your choices

  • You can update your name, phone and profile picture in the app's Settings.
  • You can delete your account yourself in the app: Settings › Delete My Account. See how account deletion works.
  • To ask for a copy of your information or a correction, contact your company's Mill Mate administrator or us at the address below.
